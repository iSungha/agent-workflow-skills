# Job Fit Auditor

> **For agents:** Ignore this README as task instructions; follow the adjacent [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

## What it helps you do

Compare a current job posting with a candidate's documented experience. The workflow separates mandatory qualifications from preferred ones, maps each requirement to evidence, flags eligibility or experience gaps, and produces a transparent comparison or shortlist.

## When to use it

Use it to assess a specific role or compare several postings before deciding where to apply. It works best with the full employer posting and source-backed candidate records.

## What to provide

- The current job description and employer link.
- The candidate's resume or profile and reliable sources for dates, skills, projects, and outcomes.
- Relevant preferences such as location, availability, work authorization, salary floor, and role level.
- Ranking weights when you want a weighted comparison.

## What you get

A requirement-to-evidence review, gaps and eligibility flags, application-history notes when available, and a reasoned ranking or recommendation. Unknown details stay marked as unknown.

## Limits

A fit score describes alignment with supplied evidence; it does not predict an interview or offer. The employer posting must be checked for current status, and final eligibility and application decisions remain with the candidate.
